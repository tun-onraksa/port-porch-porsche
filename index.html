Cost & capacity assumptions

Teaching assumptions for a hypothetical Long Beach terminal. These are not actual port tariffs.

Eight workers support each crane; two staff each truck gate; two support rail. Each occupied berth supports up to two cranes. Truck departures use capacity before rail.

Reserved resources are paid during scheduled hours, including idle time. Longer days use successive crews without overtime premiums. Rail is billed per started 100-container equivalent.

Capital costs, depreciation, demurrage and external shipping costs are excluded. Cargo inspection changes holding time; it has no separately modeled fee.
