    How PortTable works

    • Ships wait at anchor, unload at four berths, sit in the container yard, then leave by truck through the gate to the city.
    • Life 1 (normal day): PortTable books berth windows and truck pickup slots for every terminal. We assume booked slots let the gate move 12 trucks every 10 minutes instead of 10 walk-up trucks. That assumption is the only difference between Today and "Life 1 only".
    • Life 2 (storm, 2 PM to midnight): wind closes B3 and B4. When the forecast arrives at 11 AM, PortTable switches from first come, first served to lifeline priorities agreed in advance with county emergency managers: medical, then energy, then food and water, then industry. Lifeline ships stay off the berths that will close, a lifeline ship can swap into a sheltered berth ahead of a ship with only low-tier cargo, and the yard sends lifeline cargo to the gate first. Before 11 AM the autopilot and "Life 1 only" run identically.
    • Storm drill (you decide): PortTable stops and asks before each move that changes whether a lifeline order arrives on time: declaring lifeline mode, swapping a ship into a sheltered berth, digging buried boxes out. It runs the rest of the shift both ways and suggests the option that gets more (and more critical) lifeline cargo out on time. Moves that change nothing for lifeline orders it handles itself and logs. Every call lands in the Decisions tab, where you can revisit it.
    • Dig pulls priority containers out from under the stack. Each one costs two crane moves, so digging slows everything else.
    • Forecast times in the order timeline (in italics) come from the simulation running ahead, the way a digital twin projects the rest of the shift.
    • "Cargo value" counts only delivered boxes. One box stands for a batch of containers; values are illustrative. Suggestions weigh on-time lifeline orders first (medical highest), then cargo moved.
    • Controls: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click any ship, crane, the yard, the gate, a truck or a building. Keys: Space play, T Today ↔ PortTable, S jump to storm, ← → step 10 minutes, Esc clear selection.
