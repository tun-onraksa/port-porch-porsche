MAKE THE ASSUMPTIONS VISIBLE

Cost & capacity assumptions

These editable rates and capacities are teaching assumptions, not Terminal49 measurements or Long Beach tariffs. The model assumes pickup clearance after the lag below; it does not claim the real containers are released.

Eight workers support one crane; two staff each gate lane; two support rail. Each berth supports at most two cranes. Truck departures use capacity before rail. Rail is a smooth hourly approximation with 100-container train-equivalents billed when used. No overtime premium: hours above eight represent additional crews at the same rate.

Yard space × assumed slots per acre gives storage capacity. Unloading stops when storage is full. A container counts as completed when it exits this terminal, not when delivered inland. Operating hours start at 06:00; fixed daily costs continue when closed.

Delay indicators are separate from operating cost. Capital spending, depreciation, financing, cargo value, demurrage and external shipping costs are excluded.

Historical reference only: Long Beach authority FY2024 operations/administration: $218.866m; depreciation/amortization: $162.014m. These are not added to this terminal model. Official disclosure, p.19.
